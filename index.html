My Biography

I am Michelle Idahosa, a Nigerian with a background in Biology Education and a growing passion for technology and software development. My journey has taken me through different fields, but one thing has remained constant: my curiosity to understand how things work and my desire to use what I learn to solve problems.

My first degree is in Biology Education, but along the way, I discovered another area that fascinated me: technology. I became particularly interested in how websites and digital products are designed, built, and used to solve everyday problems. That curiosity led me into frontend web development, where I began learning how to turn ideas and designs into functional websites. I have worked with technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WordPress, React.js, Tailwind CSS, Vite, Formik, Yup, and Firebase. I have continued to explore UI/UX, Figma, and other areas of technology.

Now, I am taking the next step in that journey.

Why I Joined AltSchool

I joined the AltSchool Africa School of Engineering programme because I want to move from simply building interfaces to understanding how complete software products are built. My previous learning has given me an introduction to frontend development, but I know there is much more to software engineering than what happens in the browser.

I want to develop the discipline that comes with being a software engineer: breaking problems down, thinking logically, writing maintainable code, working with other developers, and learning how to turn an idea into something people can actually use. For me, AltSchool is not just about learning another skill; it is about building the technical foundation I need to take my ideas seriously and turn them into real solutions.

My Goals

By the end of this programme, I want to have grown from someone who is learning how to build websites into a developer who can confidently contribute to building complete software products.

My goals are to:

  • Build a strong foundation in software engineering.
  • Become confident in both frontend and backend development.
  • Strengthen my understanding of JavaScript and other programming concepts.
  • Learn how databases work and how applications interact with them.
  • Understand APIs and how different software systems communicate.
  • Improve my problem-solving and debugging skills.
  • Learn how to structure, build, test, and maintain software properly.
  • Build real-world projects that solve practical problems.
  • Develop a portfolio that demonstrates what I can actually build.
  • Become comfortable collaborating with other developers.
  • Continue learning beyond the programme and keep growing as a software engineer.

Most importantly, I want to learn how to look at a problem and think:

"How can technology solve this?"
